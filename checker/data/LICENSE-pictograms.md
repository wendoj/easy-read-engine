`checker/scripts/build_symbol_index.py`. The index and the pictogram images
the app shows are covered by the ARASAAC licence below, not by the project's

What this means for this project:

- Credit the author, origin, licence and owner wherever the pictograms appear:
  the result screen footer, the exported PDF footer and the README.
- Work derived from the pictograms, including this index, is shared under the
  same licence.
- No commercial use. Anyone who reuses the project commercially must swap the
  symbol library (each index entry records its `set` and `licence`).

Pictograms flagged `violence` or `sex` in the ARASAAC data are left out of the
index.
